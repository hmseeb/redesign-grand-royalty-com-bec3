FAQ

Common questions

The things people ask us most before booking a pickup.

How is my price determined?

Pricing is based on how much space your items take up in the truck, plus how difficult they are to reach. We give you a flat quote up front — not an open-ended hourly rate — so you know the number before we load the first item. If you add items on the day of service, we’ll re-quote on the spot and get your approval first.

Do I need to move anything to the curb first?

No. Leave everything exactly where it sits. Our crew brings dollies, straps and floor protection, and we handle the stairs, tight hallways and doorways. Curbside pickups are available too if that’s simply easier for you.

How soon can you come out?

Most jobs are scheduled within 24 to 72 hours of your request, and we can often fit same-week pickups around the Neosho area. If you’re working against a closing date, inspection or move-out deadline, say so in your message and we’ll prioritize the scheduling.

What areas do you serve?

We’re based in Neosho and serve Newton County and the surrounding Four States region, including Joplin, Seneca, Granby, Monett, Diamond, Anderson and Carthage. Not sure if you’re in range? Send your address and we’ll confirm.

What can’t you take?

We can’t accept hazardous materials — wet paint, solvents, motor oil, fuel, pesticides, asbestos, medical waste or pressurized tanks. Most everything else is fair game, including appliances, mattresses, electronics and construction debris. If you’re unsure about a specific item, just ask before booking.

What happens to my stuff after you haul it?

Landfill is our last option, not our first. Furniture and household goods in usable condition are routed to local donation, appliances and scrap metal go to recyclers, and cardboard is recycled. Only what genuinely can’t be reused is disposed of through proper transfer facilities.

Do you handle full property and estate cleanouts?

Yes — from a single cluttered room to an entire house, garage, barn and shed. For estate work we move at your pace, set aside anything that looks personal or valuable for your review, and can coordinate directly with realtors or executors when you’d rather not manage the details.

Can you work with contractors and property managers on an ongoing basis?

Absolutely. We run recurring and on-call pickups for remodelers, landlords, property managers and retail locations, including mid-project debris hauls and after-hours service where a daytime truck would get in the way. Mention your volume in the form and we’ll set up a standing arrangement.
